Why Go Solar

Premium Solar Is More Than Just Panels

A properly designed home solar system protects your family from rising energy costs, grid instability, and adds lasting value to your most important asset.

Grid Failure Protection

Texas storms and grid strain won't leave you in the dark. Our battery backup systems automatically switch over in milliseconds, keeping your lights on, refrigerator running, and AC cooling when the grid goes down.

Zero Electric Bills

Our systems are engineered to produce 100% or more of your annual electricity usage. Say goodbye to unpredictable rate hikes from Texas utility companies and hello to predictable $0 bills.

Property Value Increase

Homes with owned solar systems sell for an average of 4.1% more according to Zillow. A premium solar installation is one of the smartest investments you can make in your Texas property.

Federal Tax Credit

Take advantage of the 30% Federal Solar Investment Tax Credit before potential policy changes. Most Texas homeowners save $8,000–$15,000 on their system cost through this federal incentive.

25-Year Full Warranty

Every Texas Sun Power installation includes our comprehensive 25-year warranty covering panels, inverters, labor, and roof penetration. We stand behind our work for a quarter century.

Texas-Optimized Design

Our engineers design every system specifically for Texas sun angles, heat conditions, and weather patterns. We use high-temperature-rated panels that maintain efficiency even during 105°F summer days.

How It Works

From Consultation to Savings in Four Steps

We handle everything from design to permits — your only job is to enjoy the savings.

01

Free Roof Analysis

Submit your address and electric bill details. Our engineers analyze your roof using satellite imaging to determine solar viability, sun exposure, and optimal panel placement — all at no cost to you.

02

Custom System Design

We design a system tailored to your home's architecture and your family's energy consumption patterns. You receive a detailed proposal with production estimates, savings projections, and fixed pricing.

03

Professional Installation

Our in-house, licensed Texas electricians handle the entire installation. Most residential systems are fully operational within 1–3 days. We manage all permits, inspections, and utility interconnection paperwork.

04

Start Saving Immediately

Once your system is activated, you'll see savings from day one. Monitor your production in real-time through our mobile app and watch your electric meter spin backwards.

Average timeline: 3–4 weeks from consultation to activation

Our streamlined process means you can start saving within a month of your initial inquiry. Permitting timelines vary by municipality.
